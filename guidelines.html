Creative Community Guidelines

Welcome to our creative space! This is a safe and inspiring community where artists of all abilities can share their work, connect with others, and grow together. To keep our space welcoming and supportive, we’ve put together a few simple guidelines:

  1. Be Kind and Respectful

    Treat every member with kindness and respect. Celebrate each other’s creativity, offer encouragement, and avoid negative or hurtful comments. Every artist’s journey is unique.

  2. Share Your Art Safely

    Post your artwork thoughtfully. Make sure you have the rights to share it, and respect others’ creations. Give credit when you’re inspired by someone else’s work.

  3. Foster Inclusivity

    This is a space for everyone. Avoid language or behavior that might exclude or hurt others based on ability, background, identity, or experience.

  4. Encourage Growth

    Offer constructive feedback if requested, and embrace learning from others. Celebrate progress, not perfection!

  5. Protect Privacy

    Respect the privacy of fellow members. Don’t share personal information or work without permission.

  6. Keep the Space Positive and Creative

    Share your ideas, experiments, and inspirations! We want this community to be uplifting, collaborative, and full of imagination.

Together, we create a safe, inclusive, and inspiring space for every artist.
